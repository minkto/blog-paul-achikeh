---
title: Software Developer at the dawn of AI
description: As we enter the new world of AI driven development, what does this mean for software developers.
---
I have recently been developing a project with the intention of utilizing the AI tools that have currently been developed. Human nature can sometimes be the greatest obstacle to change, and I would admit, I have only started slowly incorporating AI into my life. However I for one have accepted, this is a new tool that will be here to stay and have accepted there is no going back now. 

As we approach this new dawn of AI powered software tools, everyone is racing to understand how this can be used to create robust software, while maintaining efficiency and optimizing returns. Businesses are spending substantial amounts of money to see if AI can be used to save time and money on processes that could not be further streamlined. They are also experimenting with using AI to try and make assistive automation better then what has previously been something that needed more hard coded decisions. A strong business will always try to find a competitive advantage to gain the most market share in their specific domains. It is safe to say no-one wants to be left behind.

Creating good software that is user friendly, stable and architecturally adaptable to change is HARD. I started at a time in which the best AI assistance was some IntelliSense which in retrospect was not that long ago. To create features or fix bugs, I needed to rely on a vast array of resources such as documentation, colleagues, friends, courses, books and forums. I have had many successes and of course many failures in my career. This has been essential to learn and understand how to write good software. As of this date, there is a lot of controversy in the sense of can AI essentially replace all this with just a few prompts?

These are some of the questions I have been thinking of lately,
as I myself am adapting to this new norm. 
- How feasible is "Vibe Coding"?
- Can AI completely replace all that I currently do as a developer?
- What are the limitations of AI currently?
- How can I use AI to be a better developer?
- How much can I trust the output of AI?
- What are the short-term gains and long-term gains?
- What will be the positive/negative effect of constant usage of AI?

I hope to document my journey, with the intention of learning and spreading the outcomes of what I learn online. If it will be helpful in shaping how we develop software in this new world, I believe that would be a good objective.

The AI tooling will probably change a lot, as do all tools when they are first created. However as new adaptations are made; there may still be glimmers of knowledge that will still persist and hold weight through the iterations.
